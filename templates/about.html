Meet the team.

Gary Zhuo

Luxury Real Estate Advisor

CEA: R030383Z

Sentosa Cove, Prestigious Condominiums & Landed Specialist

  • Since 2012
  • Sentosa Specialist
  • Award-Winning Realtor

Gary Zhuo has been in the real estate industry since 2012, building his career around a simple belief: that every client deserves commitment, sound advice and someone who will go the extra mile to deliver results. Over the years, he has established a strong presence in Singapore's luxury residential market, specialising in prestigious condominiums and landed properties across the Core Central Region (CCR) and Rest of Central Region (RCR).

A key area of Gary's expertise is Sentosa Cove, where he has built extensive knowledge of its unique waterfront residential market and transacted numerous homes across Sentosa, giving him practical insight into the different developments, layouts, positioning and buyer profiles that guide homeowners, buyers and investors through this distinctive luxury market. This performance has earned him recognition from the start of his career, including Top Rookie and multiple Top Achiever awards.

Before entering real estate, Gary spent 10 years serving in the Singapore Armed Forces, where discipline, resilience and leadership became an integral part of who he is today. He was recognised as a Distinguished Honour Graduate at the School of Infantry Specialists and received the Best Trainee award at the Platoon Sergeant Course, with service that took him beyond Singapore to Timor-Leste on a United Nations peacekeeping mission, Kuwait for support operations and Afghanistan as part of a NATO mission, alongside other operations.

After a decade of military service, Gary embraced a completely new challenge and pursued a career in real estate. The transition may have been significant, but the values remained the same: discipline, responsibility, perseverance and delivering on a mission. He brings those qualities into every property transaction today.

Whether representing a homeowner, helping a family find the right home or advising a client on a luxury property opportunity, he believes in understanding the client's objectives first and staying personally involved throughout the entire process. For Gary, real estate is more than closing deals; it is about earning trust, building long-term relationships and being someone clients can confidently rely on for their next property move.

Sean Chen

Real Estate Advisor

CEA: R046066H

Commercial, Shophouse, Landed & GCB Specialist

  • 13 Years in Real Estate
  • 15 Years in Corporate Banking
  • Investment & Property Advisory

With 13 years of real estate experience, Sean Chen has built a strong track record across Singapore's property market, specialising in commercial properties, shophouses, landed homes and Good Class Bungalows (GCBs), and what sets him apart is the depth of experience he brings from beyond real estate.

Prior to his property career, Sean spent 15 years in corporate banking, progressing from an individual contributor to a leadership role managing a team of approximately 30 professionals, an experience that gave him extensive exposure to financial analysis, business strategy, negotiations and the needs of business owners and investors. Today, he combines this financial expertise with his real estate experience to offer clients a more comprehensive perspective when evaluating opportunities, considering the bigger picture from value and potential to financial considerations and longer-term objectives.

His experience across both residential and commercial real estate allows him to advise a diverse clientele, from families searching for their ideal landed home to investors and business owners exploring commercial and investment properties. Sean believes that every successful property decision starts with understanding the client's priorities.

His approach is grounded in professionalism, careful analysis and practical advice, with a focus on helping clients make well-informed decisions with confidence. For Sean, the objective is not simply to close a transaction, but to become a trusted long-term advisor whom clients can rely on throughout their property journey.

Joyce Sim

Entrepreneur | Luxury Real Estate Specialist

CEA: R063524G

GCB & Landed Advisor

  • $650M+ Transacted
  • Multiple GCB Sales
  • Top 1% Performer

Entrepreneurship has been part of Joyce Sim's life since the age of 20. Beginning with her family business before venturing out on her own, she has spent decades building and managing businesses across multiple sectors, including serviced apartments, student accommodation with a portfolio of approximately 3,000 beds, childcare, manufacturing and property management. With property playing such an integral role throughout her journey, moving into real estate was a natural progression.

Since becoming a realtor in 2021, Joyce has quickly established herself in Singapore's luxury property market, specialising in Good Class Bungalows (GCBs), landed homes and high-end residential properties. Within just six months of starting her real estate career she achieved over $1 million in commissions, going on to become SRI's #1 Top Producer in 2022 and consistently ranking among the Top 1% of performers within the company to date.

Today, Joyce has clocked more than $650 million in property sales, including multiple GCB transactions and numerous luxury landed homes across Singapore's prime districts. She has also established a strong track record in the luxury leasing market, handling residential leases exceeding $170,000 per month.

Beyond the numbers, Joyce believes her greatest value lies in understanding her clients. As an entrepreneur, business owner and landed homeowner herself, she approaches property from multiple perspectives: as a home, an investment and a long-term asset. Her extensive business experience, resourcefulness and network allow her to assist clients well beyond the transaction.

Her philosophy is simple: listen, understand, find solutions, deliver results. This approach has earned Joyce the trust of both local and international clients, with much of her business coming through repeat clients and referrals. For Joyce, real estate is ultimately about more than buying and selling property; it is about building lasting relationships through trust, experience, discretion and results.

Lynn Sim

Real Estate Advisor

CEA: R056904Z

Landed & Residential Specialist

  • Entrepreneurial Experience
  • Landed Property Specialist
  • Always There For You

A graduate of the University of Queensland with a Bachelor of Commerce, Lynn Sim brings together years of entrepreneurial experience, property management expertise and a genuine passion for helping people with their property journey, an experience with property that began long before she became a real estate professional.

Coming from an entrepreneurial family, Lynn joined the family business after graduating in 2001, managing office and warehouse spaces that the business leased, renovated and sub-leased to other businesses, including spaces tendered from the Singapore Land Authority. In 2003 she ventured into a new segment, establishing a student accommodation division, followed by an expansion into serviced apartments in 2006 and hotel management in 2008.

Over the next decade, Lynn helped grow the businesses to an impressive scale, managing approximately 3,000 student accommodation beds across six locations, around 250 serviced apartment units, and the operations of a 130-room hotel in Jalan Besar. Her entrepreneurial journey then continued into the early childhood sector in 2013, where she established and expanded a preschool business to two locations before eventually selling it.

With property having been a constant throughout her career, becoming a real estate advisor was a natural next chapter. As a former landed property owner herself, Lynn developed a particular affinity for the landed market and has since transacted landed homes across Districts 10, 11, 15, 19, 20 and 21, working with both private homeowners and property developers, while also advising clients across private residential homes, condominiums and investment properties.

Her experience has also shaped the way she works. Lynn is known for being hands-on, approachable and highly committed to her clients. She believes in understanding what matters to them first, then providing practical advice and staying closely involved throughout the buying, selling or leasing process. Her ability to market properties, negotiate and structure transactions is complemented by something she values even more: building relationships that continue beyond the transaction.

Working closely alongside her sister, Joyce Sim, Lynn brings complementary experience to their partnership, combining their extensive backgrounds in business, property and the luxury residential market. Her performance has earned her recognition among the agency's leading performers, including achieving Ascending Millionaire status. But for Lynn, success has never been measured by awards alone, but by the trust clients place in her and knowing they can reach out whenever they need advice or assistance. That philosophy is best summed up in the way she has always approached her clients: Always There For You.

S Million Dollar Property is a real estate team under Singapore Realtors Inc. (SRI), focused on helping clients navigate Singapore’s residential property market through professional property advisory, marketing, and transaction services.

The team brings together a group of real estate professionals who work collaboratively to provide clients with a structured and personalised approach to property buying, selling, and investment. With the support and resources of SRI, SS Million Dollar Property aims to combine market knowledge, property expertise, and effective marketing strategies to help clients make informed decisions.

Over time, the team has built its presence within Singapore’s competitive real estate industry by focusing on strong client relationships and professional service. Its work covers various aspects of the property journey, from understanding a client’s requirements and identifying suitable properties to marketing properties and supporting clients throughout the transaction process.

What sets SS Million Dollar Property apart is its team-based approach. Rather than operating individually, the team works together by sharing knowledge, market insights, resources, and experience. This allows its members to support one another while providing clients with a more comprehensive property service.

The team also places importance on digital and social media marketing as part of its approach to reaching property buyers and sellers. Through online platforms and property content, the team is able to showcase properties, communicate market information, and engage with a wider audience.

At its core, SS Million Dollar Property represents a collaborative real estate team that combines property expertise, marketing, and client advisory to serve the Singapore property market, supported by the wider network and resources of SRI.
